Promises Kept

A comprehensive record of the bold reforms and initiatives implemented by
the Tinubu administration under the Renewed Hope Agenda.

Additional Promises Delivered

Further reforms and initiatives the Tinubu administration has advanced across the economy,
infrastructure, health, education, and security sectors.

01

Comprehensive Overhaul of the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)

Through targeted structural and operational reforms, the National Youth Service Corps is being revamped to enhance corps members' welfare, improve skill acquisition programs, and maximize the scheme's impact on national development.

02

Empowering Higher Education via Zero-Interest Student Loans

To break down financial barriers to tertiary education, the administration launched a dedicated zero-interest student loan scheme, ensuring that qualifying Nigerian students can pursue higher education without financial constraint.

03

Boosting Small Businesses: The Presidential Conditional Grant Scheme

Recognizing SMEs as the backbone of the economy, the administration established the Presidential Conditional Grant Scheme to deliver direct financial grants to small business owners, driving grassroots economic revitalization.

04

National Emergency Action on Food Insecurity

To swiftly tackle rising food costs and supply challenges, the administration declared a State of Emergency on food insecurity, aligning security, agricultural, and logistics resources to ensure food availability nationwide.

05

Foreign Exchange Unification and Market Normalization

By unifying the foreign exchange windows and eliminating arbitrage opportunities, the government has restored clarity, improved foreign direct investment inflow, and laid the groundwork for long-term currency stability.

06

Transforming Transportation: 440 New Roads and Superhighway Networks

Infrastructure development remains a cornerstone of the national agenda, highlighted by 440 new road construction projects and 2,700 kilometers of strategic superhighways designed to accelerate commerce across regions.

07

Decentralizing the Power Sector via the 2023 Electricity Act

The historic signing and implementation of the 2023 Electricity Act removed federal monopolies, granting state governments and private entities the legal mandate to generate, transmit, and distribute electricity independently.

08

Expanding Grid Capacity through Public-Private Partnerships

Leveraging private sector investment and government collaboration, over 620 megawatts of additional electricity have been integrated into the national grid to bolster industrial and domestic power supply.

09

Strategic Vision: Adding 6.3 GW to the National Grid by 2030

The newly introduced National Integrated Electricity Policy outlines a comprehensive technical and financial framework aimed at expanding grid generation capacity by 6.3 gigawatts over the coming years.

10

Strengthening Healthcare Leadership: National Health Fellowship Program

Designed to build capacity within the public health sector, the National Health Fellowship Program equips young health professionals with leadership, governance, and management skills necessary to upgrade clinical delivery across Nigeria.

11

Reduction of Car Import Levies and Duties

To ease transport costs and stimulate commercial mobility, the Federal Government significantly slashed tariff rates and import levies on vehicles entering Nigerian ports.

12

Zero Tariffs on Pharmaceutical Raw Materials

In a decisive effort to reduce drug prices and encourage local manufacturing, tariffs and duties on imported active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and medical raw materials have been completely removed.

13

Generating 2.5 Million Jobs Through LEEP

The Labour Employment Empowerment Programme (LEEP) utilizes modern digital training and remote work infrastructure to create over 2.5 million job opportunities for young Nigerians across various economic sectors.

14

Optimizing Efficiency at National Maritime Ports

Through digital integration and administrative streamlining, port operations have been modernized to eliminate bottlenecks, reduce demurrage costs, and facilitate faster cargo clearance.

15

Restoring Domestic Refining Capacity

Through comprehensive turnaround maintenance and targeted capital investments, state-owned refineries are experiencing significant operational revamps to bolster local fuel processing capacity.

16

Clean and Affordable Transportation: The National CNG Program

The Presidential Compressed Natural Gas Initiative provides a cost-effective alternative to conventional fuels through vehicle conversion kits and the deployment of CNG-fueled mass transit buses nationwide.

17

Substantial Increase in State Federal Allocations

Following key economic policy reforms, statutory monthly allocations to state governments increased by 300%, granting sub-national leaders greater financial capacity to execute localized infrastructure and social projects.

18

Crude Oil Output Rebounds to 1.8 Million BPD

Enhanced maritime security, anti-crude theft measures, and renewed operational efficiency have propelled national crude oil production from below 1 million barrels per day to 1.8 million barrels per day.

19

Decisive Action in Hostage Rescue Operations

Prioritizing the protection of human life, coordinated counter-insurgency and intelligence operations have safely rescued over 1,000 held individuals across volatile areas.

20

Comprehensive Modernization of Military Hardware

The Armed Forces have received significant procurements of advanced land vehicles, naval vessels, aerial platforms, and tactical weaponry to decisively counter security threats nationwide.

21

Integrating Local Vigilantes into Community Security Architecture

To complement formal security forces, structured frameworks have been introduced to integrate vetted community vigilante groups into national policing protocols, strengthening intelligence gathering and local safety.

22

Fostering Synergy Among Security Agencies

Breakthroughs in national defense have been accelerated through institutionalized inter-agency intelligence sharing and joint operational planning among armed forces, police, and paramilitary bodies.

23

Duty-Free Importation for Essential Food Items

To address short-term market inflation and stabilize food prices, the Federal Government temporarily removed tariffs and import duties on select basic food commodities entering the country.

24

Modernizing Aviation Infrastructure and Navigational Aids

Comprehensive infrastructure upgrades, runway rehabilitations, and advanced navigational systems have been installed across federal airports to meet international safety and operational standards.

25

Foreign Capital Importation Rises to $1.088 Billion

Reflecting renewed international confidence in key economic reforms, Nigeria's foreign capital importation rose by 66.27% quarter-on-quarter in Q4 2023, climbing from $654.65 million in Q3 2023 to $1.088 billion.

26

Reforming Social Investment Frameworks for Maximum Impact

Through structural amendments integrated into the Renewed Hope Initiative, the National Social Investments Programme (NSIP) has been restructured to enhance transparency, targeting accuracy, and disbursement speed to vulnerable households.

27

Targeted Policy Interventions to Retain Healthcare Talent

To stem the loss of skilled healthcare professionals to international opportunities, dedicated welfare incentives, career advancement pathways, and hospital modernization programs have been instituted across federal health facilities.
